🧠 Important Concepts

1. Central Government

The Central Government is the Union Government of India. It exercises powers assigned to the Union by the Constitution.
2. Union Executive

The Union Executive consists mainly of the President, Vice-President, Prime Minister and Council of Ministers.
3. President

The President is the constitutional head of the Union.
4. Election of President

The President is elected indirectly by an electoral college consisting of elected representatives as provided by the Constitution.
5. Qualifications of President

A candidate for President must be a citizen of India and must satisfy the constitutional qualifications for the office.
6. Powers of President

The President exercises executive, legislative, financial, judicial and emergency-related constitutional powers.
7. Vice-President

The Vice-President is the second highest constitutional office and serves as the ex-officio Chairman of the Rajya Sabha.
8. Prime Minister

The Prime Minister is the head of the Council of Ministers and plays a central role in the Union executive.
9. Council of Ministers

The Council of Ministers assists and advises the President and is collectively responsible to the Lok Sabha.
10. Parliament

The Parliament of India consists of the President, Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha.
11. Lok Sabha

Lok Sabha is the House of the People. Its members are directly elected by the people.
12. Rajya Sabha

Rajya Sabha is the Council of States. It represents the States and Union Territories in Parliament.
13. Speaker of Lok Sabha

The Speaker presides over the proceedings of the Lok Sabha.
14. Parliamentary Procedures

Parliamentary procedures include Question Hour, Zero Hour, motions, discussions and voting.
15. Question Hour

Question Hour provides an opportunity for members of Parliament to ask questions to ministers.
16. Zero Hour

Zero Hour is used by members to raise matters of public importance without the usual advance notice required for questions.
17. Money Bill

A Money Bill deals with matters specified by the Constitution relating to taxation, government funds and public expenditure.
18. Speaker and Money Bill

The Speaker of the Lok Sabha decides whether a Bill is a Money Bill or not.
19. Budget

The Union Budget presents the government's estimated receipts and expenditure for a financial year.
20. Supreme Court

The Supreme Court is the highest court in India's judicial system.
21. Judicial Review

Judicial review allows courts to examine the constitutional validity of laws and government actions.
22. Judicial Activism

Judicial activism refers to an active role played by the judiciary in protecting constitutional values and rights.
23. Public Interest Litigation

Public Interest Litigation allows matters affecting public interest to be brought before courts.
