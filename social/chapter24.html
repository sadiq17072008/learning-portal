🧠 Important Concepts

1. Globalization

Globalization refers to the increasing integration and interdependence of economies, markets, businesses and people across countries.
2. International Trade

International trade is the exchange of goods and services between different countries.
3. Export

An export is a good or service sold by one country to another country.
4. Import

An import is a good or service purchased by a country from another country.
5. Foreign Trade

Foreign trade refers to buying and selling goods and services between countries.
6. Liberalization

Liberalization means reducing restrictions and controls on economic activities and trade.
7. Privatization

Privatization involves increasing the role of private ownership and participation in economic activities.
8. Multinational Corporation

A multinational corporation is a company that operates in more than one country.
9. Foreign Direct Investment

Foreign direct investment refers to investment made by a person or company from one country in a business located in another country.
10. WTO

WTO stands for World Trade Organization. It provides a framework for international trade between countries.
11. Trade Barrier

A trade barrier is a restriction placed on international trade, such as tariffs or quotas.
12. Tariff

A tariff is a tax imposed on imported goods.
13. Balance of Trade

Balance of trade is the difference between the value of a country's exports and imports of goods.
14. Trade Surplus

A trade surplus occurs when the value of exports is greater than the value of imports.
15. Trade Deficit

A trade deficit occurs when the value of imports is greater than the value of exports.
16. Global Market

A global market allows goods, services, investment and businesses to operate across national borders.

🧮 Important Formulas

Balance of Trade = Value of Exports − Value of Imports
Trade Surplus = Exports − Imports when Exports > Imports
Trade Deficit = Imports − Exports when Imports > Exports
Export Share = (Exports ÷ Total Trade) × 100
Import Share = (Imports ÷ Total Trade) × 100
Total Trade = Exports + Imports

🔢 Numerical Sums

Example 1: Exports = ₹500 crore
Imports = ₹400 crore

Balance of Trade = 500 − 400
= ₹100 crore surplus
Example 2: Exports = ₹300 crore
Imports = ₹450 crore

Trade deficit = 450 − 300
= ₹150 crore
Example 3: Exports = ₹600 crore
Imports = ₹400 crore

Total trade = 600 + 400
= ₹1,000 crore

= 60%
Example 5: Imports = ₹400 crore

🌍 Global Trade Flow

Globalization connects producers, consumers and businesses across countries. Goods and services can move across borders through international trade.

🇮🇳 Country A → Exports → 🌍 International Market
🌍 International Market → Imports → 🇮🇳 Country A
🏢 Multinational Companies → Investment → 🌎 Different Countries
