🧠 Important Concepts

1. Climate

Climate refers to the average weather conditions of a place over a long period.
2. Factors Affecting Climate

India's climate is affected by latitude, altitude, distance from the sea, relief features, monsoon winds and jet streams.
3. Latitude

India extends approximately from 8°4'N to 37°6'N. The Tropic of Cancer divides the country into two nearly equal halves.
4. Altitude

Temperature decreases as altitude increases. The normal lapse rate is about 6.5°C for every 1000 metres of ascent.
5. Distance from the Sea

Coastal areas experience a more moderate climate, while areas farther from the sea generally experience greater temperature variations.
6. Relief Features

The Himalayas influence India's climate by preventing cold continental winds from entering much of the country and helping monsoon winds produce rainfall.
7. Jet Streams

Jet streams are high-speed winds in the upper atmosphere that influence weather and the Indian monsoon.
8. Indian Monsoon

India has a tropical monsoon climate. Seasonal reversal of wind direction is an important characteristic of the monsoon.
9. South-West Monsoon

The south-west monsoon brings the major rainy season to most parts of India.
10. North-East Monsoon

The north-east monsoon is especially important for rainfall over Tamil Nadu and parts of south-eastern India.
11. Cold Weather Season

The cold weather season generally occurs from December to February.
12. Hot Weather Season

The hot weather season generally occurs from March to May.
13. South-West Monsoon Season

The south-west monsoon season generally extends from June to September.
14. Retreating Monsoon

The retreating monsoon season generally occurs during October and November.
15. Rainfall Distribution

Rainfall is unevenly distributed across India because of differences in relief, wind direction and other climatic factors.
16. Tropical Evergreen Forests

These forests occur in areas receiving high rainfall and remain green throughout the year.
17. Tropical Deciduous Forests

These forests are also called monsoon forests and shed their leaves during the dry season.
18. Tropical Thorn Forests

Thorn forests occur mainly in regions with low rainfall and dry conditions.
19. Mountain Forests

Mountain vegetation changes with altitude because temperature and moisture conditions change as elevation increases.
20. Mangrove Forests

Mangrove vegetation occurs in tidal coastal areas, particularly around river deltas.
21. Wildlife

India's varied climate and vegetation support diverse wildlife including mammals, birds, reptiles and other species.
22. Climatic Regions

India's climatic regions vary according to temperature, rainfall, altitude and geographical location.

🔢 Important Numerical Facts
