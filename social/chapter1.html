🧠 Important Concepts

1. Nationalism

Nationalism created intense competition among European nations and encouraged nations to expand their power and influence.
2. Imperialism

European powers competed for colonies, markets and resources. This rivalry increased tension among the major powers.
3. Militarism

European countries increased military expenditure, expanded their armies and navies, and prepared themselves for war.
4. Triple Alliance

The Triple Alliance consisted of Germany, Austria-Hungary and Italy.
5. Triple Entente

The Triple Entente consisted of Britain, France and Russia.
6. Immediate Cause

The assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand of Austria-Hungary at Sarajevo on 28 June 1914 became the immediate cause of the First World War.
7. Treaty of Versailles

The Treaty of Versailles was signed in 1919 and imposed severe terms on Germany after the First World War.
8. League of Nations

The League of Nations was established after World War I with the objective of maintaining international peace.
9. Major Consequences

The war caused enormous loss of life, economic destruction, political changes and the collapse of several empires.

🗓️ Important Events

1914: Assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand at Sarajevo and outbreak of World War I.
1915: Italy entered the war on the side of the Allied Powers.
1917: The United States entered the war. Russia also underwent revolution and subsequently withdrew from the war.
1918: Germany agreed to an armistice, bringing the fighting to an end.
1919: Treaty of Versailles was signed.
1920: The League of Nations began functioning.
