🧠 Important Concepts

1. Post-War Problems

The First World War left Europe with enormous economic difficulties, political instability, unemployment and social tensions.
2. Russian Revolution

The Russian Revolution of 1917 resulted in the overthrow of the Tsarist government and eventually led to the establishment of a communist government.
3. Fascism

Fascism developed in Italy under Benito Mussolini. It promoted dictatorship, extreme nationalism and militarism.
4. Benito Mussolini

Benito Mussolini became the leader of Fascist Italy and established a dictatorial regime.
5. Nazism

Nazism developed in Germany under Adolf Hitler. It promoted dictatorship, extreme nationalism, militarism and racial ideology.
6. Adolf Hitler

Adolf Hitler became the leader of the Nazi Party and later established a dictatorship in Germany.
7. Great Depression

The Great Depression was a severe worldwide economic crisis that began in 1929 and resulted in unemployment, poverty and industrial decline.
8. Dictatorships

Economic crisis and political instability contributed to the rise of authoritarian governments in several countries.
9. Road to World War II

Aggressive nationalism, militarism, territorial expansion and the weakness of collective security contributed to the outbreak of the Second World War.

🗓️ Important Events

1917: Russian Revolution transformed the political situation in Russia.
1919: Treaty of Versailles was signed and the Weimar Republic was established in Germany.
1922: Mussolini came to power in Italy.
1929: The Great Depression began with the Wall Street Crash and spread internationally.
1933: Adolf Hitler became Chancellor of Germany and the Nazi dictatorship developed.
1930s: Aggressive expansion by dictatorships increased international tensions.
