🧠 Important Concepts

1. Poligar Resistance

The Poligars resisted the expansion of British authority in Tamil regions and opposed the collection of revenue under British control.
2. Veerapandiya Kattabomman

Veerapandiya Kattabomman, the Poligar of Panchalankurichi, resisted British authority and became an important symbol of resistance.
3. Maruthu Brothers

Periya Maruthu and Chinna Maruthu played an important role in the resistance against British rule in southern Tamil Nadu.
4. Vellore Mutiny

The Vellore Mutiny of 1806 was one of the earliest major military uprisings against British rule in India.
5. South Indian Rebellion

The South Indian Rebellion was an important phase of early resistance against British authority in the southern regions.
6. Swadeshi Movement

The Swadeshi movement encouraged Indians to use indigenous products and oppose British economic domination.
7. V.O. Chidambaranar

V.O. Chidambaranar played an important role in the Swadeshi movement and founded the Swadeshi Steam Navigation Company.
8. Swadeshi Steam Navigation Company

The company was established to challenge British control over coastal shipping and encourage Indian enterprise.
9. Subramania Bharati

Subramania Bharati used poetry and journalism to promote nationalism, freedom and social reform.
10. Subramania Siva

Subramania Siva was a prominent nationalist who spread the message of freedom through speeches and public activities.
11. Home Rule Movement

The Home Rule Movement demanded self-government for India and attracted nationalist support in different parts of the country.
12. Annie Besant

Annie Besant played an important role in the Home Rule Movement and nationalist politics.
13. Non-Cooperation Movement

The Non-Cooperation Movement encouraged people to withdraw cooperation from British institutions.
14. Civil Disobedience Movement

The Civil Disobedience Movement encouraged people to peacefully break unjust colonial laws.
15. Vedaranyam Salt March

C. Rajagopalachari led the Vedaranyam Salt March in Tamil Nadu during the Civil Disobedience Movement.
16. Tiruppur Kumaran

Tiruppur Kumaran became a symbol of patriotism and sacrifice during the freedom struggle.
17. S. Satyamurti

S. Satyamurti was an important Congress leader from Tamil Nadu who worked for India's freedom.
18. K. Kamaraj

K. Kamaraj participated in the national movement and became an important leader from Tamil Nadu.
19. Quit India Movement

The Quit India Movement of 1942 demanded an immediate end to British rule in India.
20. Role of Tamil Nadu

Tamil Nadu contributed significantly to the Indian freedom struggle through political movements, journalism, economic resistance, protests and mass participation.

🗓️ Important Dates and Events

1799: Veerapandiya Kattabomman was executed after his resistance against British authority.
1801: The Maruthu Brothers issued a famous proclamation calling for resistance against British rule.
1806: Vellore Mutiny took place.
1906: V.O. Chidambaranar founded the Swadeshi Steam Navigation Company.
1907: The nationalist activities of V.O.C., Subramania Bharati and Subramania Siva gained importance in Tamil Nadu.
1916: The Home Rule Movement became an important phase of the nationalist struggle.
1920: The Non-Cooperation Movement was launched.
1930: The Civil Disobedience Movement and Vedaranyam Salt March took place.
1932: Civil disobedience activities continued despite colonial repression.
1942: Quit India Movement was launched.
1947: India became independent.
