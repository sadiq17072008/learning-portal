🧠 Important Concepts

1. World War II

World War II began in 1939 and ended in 1945. It involved countries across Europe, Asia and Africa and caused enormous military and civilian casualties.
2. Treaty of Versailles

The Treaty of Versailles was signed in June 1919. Germany was required to give up territories, reduce its armed forces and pay reparations.
3. Failure of the League of Nations

The League of Nations was intended to maintain international peace, but it lacked sufficient power to prevent aggressive actions by major powers.
4. Rise of Adolf Hitler

Adolf Hitler exploited German economic and political dissatisfaction. He became Chancellor in 1933 and established a dictatorship.
5. German Rearmament

Hitler violated the restrictions of the Treaty of Versailles by rebuilding and expanding German armed forces.
6. Axis Powers

Germany, Italy and Japan formed the major Axis alliance during World War II.
7. Allied Powers

Britain, France, the Soviet Union and the United States were among the major Allied powers that fought the Axis.
8. Munich Pact

The Munich Agreement of 1938 allowed Germany to acquire the Sudetenland from Czechoslovakia.
9. Invasion of Poland

Germany invaded Poland on 1 September 1939. Britain and France subsequently declared war on Germany.
10. Pearl Harbor

Japan attacked the United States naval base at Pearl Harbor on 7 December 1941. This led to the entry of the United States into World War II.
11. Operation Barbarossa

Germany launched Operation Barbarossa against the Soviet Union in June 1941.
12. End of the War

Germany surrendered in May 1945. Japan formally surrendered on 2 September 1945.

🗓️ Important Events

1919: Treaty of Versailles was signed.
1933: Adolf Hitler became Chancellor of Germany.
1935: Italy invaded Ethiopia.
1936: The Rome-Berlin Axis was formed.
1938: Germany annexed Austria and later acquired the Sudetenland through the Munich Agreement.
1 September 1939: Germany invaded Poland.
1940: France fell and the Battle of Britain took place.
June 1941: Germany launched Operation Barbarossa against the Soviet Union.
7 December 1941: Japan attacked Pearl Harbor.
1944: Allied forces landed in Normandy on D-Day.
May 1945: Germany surrendered.
August 1945: Atomic bombs were dropped on Hiroshima and Nagasaki.
2 September 1945: Japan formally surrendered, bringing World War II to an end.
