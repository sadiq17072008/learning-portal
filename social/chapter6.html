🧠 Important Concepts

1. Palayams

Palayams were territorial divisions associated with the Palayakkarar system. The Palayakkarars were local chieftains who exercised authority over their territories.
2. Palayakkarars

The Palayakkarars resisted the expansion of East India Company authority when the Company attempted to consolidate its power.
3. Puli Thevar

Puli Thevar of Nerkattumseval was one of the earliest Palayakkarars to resist the East India Company's territorial expansion.
4. Velunachiyar

Velunachiyar was the queen of Sivagangai who resisted British authority. She later regained her kingdom with support from allies.
5. Kuyili

Kuyili was an important associate of Velunachiyar and is remembered for her role in the resistance against the British.
6. Veerapandya Kattabomman

Veerapandya Kattabomman, the Palayakkarar of Panchalamkurichi, resisted the East India Company's demands and became a prominent symbol of resistance.
7. Confrontation with Jackson

Collector Jackson summoned Kattabomman to Ramanathapuram. Their confrontation became an important episode in the conflict.
8. Panchalamkurichi

Panchalamkurichi was the stronghold associated with Kattabomman and became the centre of military conflict during the revolt.
9. Marudhu Brothers

The Marudhu Brothers of Sivagangai played an important role in the resistance against British rule during 1800–1801.
10. Tiruchirappalli Proclamation

The Marudhu Brothers issued the Tiruchirappalli Proclamation calling upon people to resist British rule.
11. Dheeran Chinnamalai

Dheeran Chinnamalai was another important resistance leader who fought against British authority in the Kongu region.
12. Vellore Revolt

The Vellore Revolt broke out in 1806. It was an important early uprising by Indian soldiers against British military regulations and policies.
13. Military Regulations

New military regulations introduced by the British created resentment among Indian soldiers, including objections related to dress and religious customs.
14. Tipu Sultan's Family

The sons of Tipu Sultan were kept at Vellore Fort after the fall of Srirangapatnam and their presence became connected with the events surrounding the revolt.
15. Importance of Early Revolts

These revolts demonstrate the early resistance offered by regional powers, Palayakkarars and Indian soldiers against the expansion of British authority.

🗓️ Important Dates and Events

1755–1767: Period associated with the Revolt of Puli Thevar.
1755: Company forces under Colonel Heron moved against Puli Thevar.
1790–1799: Period associated with the rebellion of Veerapandya Kattabomman.
1799: Kattabomman was captured and executed at Kayathar.
1800–1801: Rebellion of the Marudhu Brothers.
1801: The Marudhu Brothers issued the famous Tiruchirappalli Proclamation.
1801: The Carnatic Treaty was concluded.
1805: Dheeran Chinnamalai was executed.
10 July 1806: The Vellore Revolt broke out.
