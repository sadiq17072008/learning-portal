🧠 Important Concepts

1. Food Security

Food security means that all people, at all times, have physical and economic access to sufficient, safe and nutritious food.
2. Availability

Availability means that sufficient quantities of food are present through domestic production, imports and stocks.
3. Accessibility

Accessibility means that people are able to obtain food without physical or social barriers.
4. Affordability

Affordability means that people have enough income or purchasing power to obtain adequate food.
5. Food Insecurity

Food insecurity occurs when people do not have reliable access to sufficient and nutritious food.
6. Nutrition

Nutrition is the process by which the body obtains and uses nutrients needed for growth, development, energy and health.
7. Balanced Diet

A balanced diet contains adequate amounts of carbohydrates, proteins, fats, vitamins, minerals, fibre and water.
8. Malnutrition

Malnutrition refers to an unhealthy condition caused by inadequate, excessive or imbalanced intake of nutrients.
9. Undernutrition

Undernutrition occurs when the body does not receive enough energy or essential nutrients.
10. Overnutrition

Overnutrition occurs when nutrient or energy intake is excessive.
11. Public Distribution System

The Public Distribution System, or PDS, supplies essential food commodities to eligible people through a network of fair price shops.
12. Buffer Stock

Buffer stock is the stock of food grains maintained by the government to meet shortages and support food security.
13. Minimum Support Price

Minimum Support Price is a price announced by the government to provide price support to farmers for selected agricultural products.
14. Food Corporation of India

The Food Corporation of India plays an important role in the procurement, storage and distribution of food grains.
15. Poverty and Food Security

Poverty can reduce a household's purchasing power and make it difficult to obtain adequate nutritious food.
16. Nutrition Security

Nutrition security means having access to adequate quantities of nutritious food and the conditions necessary for proper utilisation of nutrients.

🧮 Important Calculations

Food Requirement = Population × Food Requirement per Person
Per Person Availability = Total Food Available ÷ Population
Food Deficit = Required Food − Available Food
Food Surplus = Available Food − Required Food when Available Food > Required Food
Percentage Change = (Change ÷ Original Value) × 100
Per Capita Availability = Total Quantity ÷ Total Population

🔢 Numerical Sums

Example 1: A population of 1,000 people requires 2 kg of food per person.

Total requirement = 1,000 × 2
= 2,000 kg
Example 2: Total food available = 5,000 kg
Population = 2,000 people

Per person availability = 5,000 ÷ 2,000
= 2.5 kg per person
Example 3: Required food = 8,000 kg
Available food = 6,500 kg

Food deficit = 8,000 − 6,500
= 1,500 kg
Example 4: Required food = 7,000 kg
Available food = 8,500 kg

Food surplus = 8,500 − 7,000
= 1,500 kg
Example 5: A population of 5,000 people requires 3 kg per person.

Requirement = 5,000 × 3
= 15,000 kg
Example 6: Food available = 20,000 kg
Population = 5,000 people

Per person availability = 20,000 ÷ 5,000
= 4 kg
Example 7: Required food = 12,000 kg
Available food = 10,000 kg

Deficit = 12,000 − 10,000
= 2,000 kg
Example 8: Original food stock = 10,000 tonnes
New stock = 12,000 tonnes

Increase = 12,000 − 10,000
= 2,000 tonnes
Example 9: Original stock = 10,000 tonnes
Increase = 2,000 tonnes

Percentage increase = 2,000 ÷ 10,000 × 100
= 20%
Example 10: Food requirement = 25,000 kg
Food available = 30,000 kg

Surplus = 30,000 − 25,000
= 5,000 kg

🍚 Food Security System

Food security depends on the availability of food, people's ability to access it and their purchasing power to obtain it.

🌾 Production → Food is produced through agriculture.
🏢 Procurement → Government agencies can procure food grains.
🏬 Storage → Food grains can be maintained as stocks.
🛒 Distribution → Food reaches consumers through distribution systems.
👨‍👩‍👧 Consumption → Households obtain food for their nutritional needs.
