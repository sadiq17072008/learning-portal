🧠 Important Concepts

1. Foreign Policy

Foreign policy refers to the principles and strategies followed by a country in dealing with other countries and international organisations.
2. India's Foreign Policy

India's foreign policy aims to protect national interests while promoting international peace, cooperation and friendly relations.
3. Article 51

Article 51 of the Indian Constitution directs the State to promote international peace and security and maintain just and honourable relations between nations.
4. Non-Alignment

Non-Alignment means not joining military blocs and maintaining an independent approach in international affairs.
5. Non-Aligned Movement

The Non-Aligned Movement was formed by countries that wanted to remain independent of the major military blocs during the Cold War.
6. Jawaharlal Nehru

Jawaharlal Nehru played a major role in shaping India's early foreign policy and promoting the principle of Non-Alignment.
7. Panchsheel

Panchsheel refers to the Five Principles of Peaceful Co-existence. India and China signed an agreement based on these principles in 1954.
8. Five Principles of Panchsheel

The principles include mutual respect for territorial integrity and sovereignty, mutual non-aggression, mutual non-interference, equality and mutual benefit, and peaceful coexistence.
9. Afro-Asian Unity

India supported cooperation among Asian and African countries and opposed colonialism and racial discrimination.
10. World Peace

India supports peaceful settlement of disputes, international cooperation and peaceful coexistence among nations.
11. India's Relations with Neighbours

India seeks friendly and cooperative relations with neighbouring countries while protecting its national interests.
12. National Interest

National interest is an important determinant of foreign policy and includes the security, sovereignty and development of the country.
13. Racial Equality

India has opposed racial discrimination and supported equality among peoples and nations.
14. Anti-Colonialism

India has historically supported the freedom of peoples from colonial rule and promoted independence.
15. International Cooperation

India supports cooperation among nations to address common problems and promote peace and development.
