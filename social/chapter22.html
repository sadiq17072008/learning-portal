🧠 Important Concepts

1. India and International Relations

International relations refer to the relationships and interactions between India and other countries and international organisations.
2. India and Pakistan

India and Pakistan share historical, geographical and cultural connections. Their relations have also been affected by conflicts and disputes.
3. India and China

India and China are major Asian countries. Their relationship includes economic cooperation as well as boundary-related issues.
4. McMahon Line

The McMahon Line is associated with the boundary between India and China in the eastern sector.
5. India and Bangladesh

India and Bangladesh share historical, geographical and economic connections and cooperate on several issues.
6. India and Sri Lanka

India and Sri Lanka have close geographical and cultural connections and maintain diplomatic relations.
7. India and Nepal

India and Nepal share close historical, cultural and geographical relations.
8. India and Bhutan

India and Bhutan maintain friendly relations and cooperate in areas such as development and economic activities.
9. India and Myanmar

Myanmar is an important neighbouring country because of India's geographical position and its connection with South-East Asia.
10. India and United States

India and the United States cooperate in several areas including trade, technology, education and strategic relations.
11. India and Russia

India and Russia have maintained longstanding cooperation in areas including defence, energy and technology.
12. India and West Asia

India has important economic and strategic relations with countries in West Asia.
13. United Nations Organisation

India participates actively in the United Nations and supports international peace and cooperation.
14. SAARC

SAARC stands for South Asian Association for Regional Cooperation and promotes cooperation among South Asian countries.
15. ASEAN

ASEAN stands for Association of Southeast Asian Nations.
16. OPEC

OPEC stands for Organization of the Petroleum Exporting Countries.
17. BRICS

BRICS is a grouping associated with major emerging economies and promotes cooperation among its member countries.
18. International Cooperation

India works with other countries and international organisations to promote peace, development and cooperation.
