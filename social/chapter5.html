🧠 Important Concepts

1. Social Reform Movements

During the nineteenth century, several reformers and organisations worked to remove harmful social practices and promote education, equality and social progress.
2. Raja Rammohan Roy

Raja Rammohan Roy was one of the important pioneers of Indian social and religious reform. He opposed social evils and supported modern education and reform.
3. Brahmo Samaj

The Brahmo Samaj was founded by Raja Rammohan Roy and became an important centre of religious and social reform.
4. Ishwar Chandra Vidyasagar

Ishwar Chandra Vidyasagar strongly supported women's education and widow remarriage.
5. Widow Remarriage

Reformers campaigned against restrictions on widows and supported their right to remarry.
6. Swami Dayanand Saraswati

Swami Dayanand Saraswati founded the Arya Samaj and called for a return to the teachings of the Vedas.
7. Arya Samaj

Arya Samaj became an important reform movement that promoted education and social reform.
8. Swami Vivekananda

Swami Vivekananda emphasised service to humanity, education and social upliftment.
9. Ramakrishna Mission

The Ramakrishna Mission was established by Swami Vivekananda and worked in the fields of education, healthcare and social service.
10. Sir Syed Ahmad Khan

Sir Syed Ahmad Khan promoted modern education among Muslims and was associated with the Aligarh Movement.
11. Aligarh Movement

The Aligarh Movement encouraged modern education and intellectual development among Indian Muslims.
12. Jyotiba Phule

Jyotiba Phule worked for the upliftment of oppressed communities and promoted education for women and disadvantaged groups.
13. Satyashodhak Samaj

Jyotiba Phule founded the Satyashodhak Samaj to work for social equality and against caste discrimination.
14. Savitribai Phule

Savitribai Phule was a pioneering educator who worked for women's education and social equality.
15. Narayana Guru

Narayana Guru worked against caste discrimination and promoted social equality and spiritual reform.
16. Women's Education

Reformers promoted women's education as an important means of improving the position of women in Indian society.

🗓️ Important Dates and Events

1828: Raja Rammohan Roy founded the Brahmo Samaj.
1848: Jyotiba Phule and Savitribai Phule opened an important girls' school in Pune.
1856: The Hindu Widows' Remarriage Act was passed.
1875: Swami Dayanand Saraswati founded the Arya Samaj.
1875: The Aligarh movement developed under the leadership of Sir Syed Ahmad Khan.
1873: Jyotiba Phule founded the Satyashodhak Samaj.
1897: Swami Vivekananda founded the Ramakrishna Mission.
