🧠 Important Concepts

1. Location of India

India is located in the northern and eastern hemispheres. It occupies a strategic position in South Asia and is surrounded by the Arabian Sea, Bay of Bengal and Indian Ocean.
2. Latitudinal Extent

India's mainland extends from approximately 8°4'N to 37°6'N latitudes.
3. Longitudinal Extent

India's mainland extends from approximately 68°7'E to 97°25'E longitudes.
4. Standard Meridian

The Standard Meridian of India is 82°30'E longitude. Indian Standard Time is based on this meridian.
5. Himalayas

The Himalayas are young fold mountains located along India's northern boundary.
6. Northern Plains

The Northern Plains were formed mainly by the deposition of sediments brought by major Himalayan rivers.
7. Peninsular Plateau

The Peninsular Plateau is an ancient landmass composed mainly of hard rocks.
8. Indian Desert

The Indian Desert, also known as the Thar Desert, lies mainly in north-western India.
9. Coastal Plains

India has western and eastern coastal plains extending along the Arabian Sea and Bay of Bengal respectively.
10. Islands

India's major island groups include the Andaman and Nicobar Islands and the Lakshadweep Islands.
11. Drainage

Drainage refers to the river system of an area, including rivers and their tributaries.
12. Himalayan Rivers

Major Himalayan river systems include the Indus, Ganga and Brahmaputra systems.
13. Peninsular Rivers

Important Peninsular rivers include Mahanadi, Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri, Narmada and Tapi.
14. Ganga River System

The Ganga is one of India's major river systems and has numerous tributaries.
15. Brahmaputra

The Brahmaputra is a major river of north-eastern India and forms an important drainage system.
16. East-flowing Rivers

Major east-flowing Peninsular rivers include Mahanadi, Godavari, Krishna and Kaveri.
17. West-flowing Rivers

Narmada and Tapi are important west-flowing Peninsular rivers.
18. River Basin

A river basin is the area drained by a river and its tributaries.
19. Water Resources

Rivers are important sources of water for agriculture, drinking, industries and hydroelectric power.
20. Physical Diversity

India's physical landscape includes mountains, plains, plateaus, deserts, coastal regions and islands.

🔢 Important Numerical Facts

Latitudinal Extent: Approximately 8°4'N to 37°6'N.
Longitudinal Extent: Approximately 68°7'E to 97°25'E.
Standard Meridian: 82°30'E.
Time Difference: The longitudinal extent of India creates approximately a two-hour difference in local time from the western to eastern parts of the country.
Tropic of Cancer: The Tropic of Cancer is located at approximately 23°30'N latitude and passes through India.
