🧠 Important Concepts

1. Population

Population refers to the total number of people living in a particular area at a particular time.
2. Population Distribution

Population distribution refers to the way people are spread across a region. India's population is unevenly distributed because of differences in physical, economic and social factors.
3. Population Density

Population density is the number of people living in a unit area of land.
4. Population Growth

Population growth refers to the increase or decrease in population over a period of time.
5. Birth Rate

Birth rate is the number of live births per thousand population in a year.
6. Death Rate

Death rate is the number of deaths per thousand population in a year.
7. Migration

Migration is the movement of people from one place to another for temporary or permanent settlement.
8. Urbanisation

Urbanisation refers to the increasing concentration of population in towns and cities.
9. Road Transport

Road transport is useful for short and medium-distance movement of passengers and goods and provides door-to-door connectivity.
10. Rail Transport

Railways are important for transporting large numbers of passengers and bulk goods over long distances.
11. Water Transport

Water transport is especially useful for carrying bulky goods over long distances at relatively low cost.
12. Air Transport

Air transport is the fastest mode of transport and is useful for long-distance passenger travel and high-value goods.
13. Pipeline Transport

Pipelines are used to transport liquids and gases such as petroleum and natural gas.
14. Communication

Communication is the process of sharing information, ideas and messages from one person or place to another.
15. Mass Communication

Mass communication distributes information to a large number of people through media such as television, radio, newspapers and digital platforms.
16. Trade

Trade is the exchange of goods and services between people, regions or countries.
17. Import

An import is a good or service purchased from another country.
18. Export

An export is a good or service sold to another country.
19. Balance of Trade

Balance of trade is the difference between the value of a country's exports and imports of goods.
20. Ports

Ports are important centres where ships load and unload passengers and goods. They play an important role in India's international trade.

🔢 Numerical Practice

Example 1: A district has a population of 5,00,000 people and an area of 2,000 km².

Population density:

5,00,000 ÷ 2,000 = 250 persons/km²
Example 2: A town has a population of 4,00,000. If 20,000 people migrate into the town, what is the new population?

4,00,000 + 20,000 = 4,20,000
Example 3: A country exports goods worth ₹80,000 crore and imports goods worth ₹70,000 crore.

Balance of trade:

₹80,000 − ₹70,000 = ₹10,000 crore surplus
Example 4: A city has 6,00,000 people. If 10% of them migrate out, how many people leave?

6,00,000 × 10 ÷ 100 = 60,000 people
Example 5: A transport company carries 2,500 tonnes of goods per day. How much will it carry in 8 days?

2,500 × 8 = 20,000 tonnes
