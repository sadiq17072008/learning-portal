🧠 Important Concepts

1. Agriculture

Agriculture is the cultivation of crops and rearing of animals for food, raw materials and other economic purposes.
2. Importance of Agriculture

Agriculture provides food, raw materials for industries and employment and contributes to the Indian economy.
3. Primitive Subsistence Farming

This type of farming is generally practiced on small patches of land using simple tools and family labour.
4. Intensive Subsistence Farming

Intensive farming involves high levels of labour and inputs on relatively small areas of land to obtain higher production.
5. Commercial Farming

Commercial farming is mainly undertaken for producing crops for sale in the market.
6. Plantation Agriculture

Plantation agriculture is a form of commercial farming involving large-scale cultivation of crops such as tea, coffee and rubber.
7. Cropping Pattern

Cropping pattern refers to the distribution of cultivated crops over a region and the way crops are grown during different seasons.
8. Kharif Crops

Kharif crops are generally sown with the beginning of the monsoon and harvested after the rainy season. Examples include rice, maize and cotton.
9. Rabi Crops

Rabi crops are generally sown during the winter season and harvested in spring. Examples include wheat, gram and mustard.
10. Zaid Crops

Zaid crops are grown between the Rabi and Kharif seasons. Examples include watermelon and cucumber.
11. Rice

Rice is an important food crop and requires relatively high temperature and adequate water.
12. Wheat

Wheat is an important Rabi crop and requires a cool growing season and suitable moisture.
13. Millets

Millets such as jowar, bajra and ragi are important food crops and can grow in relatively dry conditions.
14. Pulses

Pulses are important sources of protein. Many pulse crops can also help improve soil fertility through nitrogen fixation.
15. Sugarcane

Sugarcane is an important commercial crop used mainly for producing sugar and related products.
16. Oilseeds

Groundnut, mustard, soybean and sunflower are examples of important oilseed crops.
17. Tea

Tea is an important plantation crop. It grows well in warm and humid conditions with suitable rainfall.
18. Coffee

Coffee is an important plantation crop cultivated mainly in suitable highland regions of southern India.
19. Cotton

Cotton is an important fibre crop and is an important raw material for the textile industry.
20. Jute

Jute is an important fibre crop that grows well in warm and humid conditions.
21. Rubber

Rubber is a plantation crop that requires a warm and humid climate with adequate rainfall.
22. Irrigation

Irrigation is the artificial supply of water to agricultural land when rainfall is insufficient.
23. Green Revolution

The Green Revolution introduced improved agricultural practices, high-yielding varieties, irrigation and modern inputs to increase crop production.
24. Agricultural Development

Agricultural development involves improving productivity, irrigation, technology, infrastructure, markets and farming practices.

🔢 Numerical Practice

Example 1: A farmer has 20 hectares of land and grows rice on 8 hectares.

Percentage of land under rice:

(8 ÷ 20) × 100 = 40%
Example 2: A farmer produces 5000 kg of wheat from 2 hectares.

Yield per hectare:

5000 ÷ 2 = 2500 kg/hectare
Example 3: A farm produces 8000 kg from 4 hectares.

Yield:

8000 ÷ 4 = 2000 kg/hectare
Example 4: A farmer's crop production increases from 4000 kg to 5000 kg.

Increase:

5000 − 4000 = 1000 kg
Example 5: Percentage increase from 4000 kg to 5000 kg:

(1000 ÷ 4000) × 100 = 25%
