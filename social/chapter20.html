🧠 Important Concepts

1. State Government

State Government is the government that administers a particular state according to the Constitution of India.
2. State Legislature

The State Legislature makes laws for matters assigned to the state under the Constitution.
3. Legislative Assembly

The Legislative Assembly is the directly elected House of the State Legislature.
4. Legislative Council

Some states have a Legislative Council as the upper House of the State Legislature.
5. Governor

The Governor is the constitutional head of a state.
6. Appointment of Governor

The Governor of a state is appointed by the President of India.
7. Qualifications of Governor

A person must be a citizen of India and must have completed 35 years of age to be appointed Governor.
8. Chief Minister

The Chief Minister is the head of the elected government in the state and leads the Council of Ministers.
9. Council of Ministers

The Council of Ministers assists the Chief Minister in administering the state.
10. Member of Legislative Assembly

Members of the Legislative Assembly are elected by the people of their respective constituencies.
11. State Judiciary

The State Judiciary includes the High Court and subordinate courts functioning within the state.
12. High Court

The High Court is the highest judicial authority at the state level.
13. Role of State Government

The State Government performs functions relating to areas such as public health, agriculture, education, police and local administration.
14. Chief Minister and Governor

The Governor is the constitutional head while the Chief Minister leads the elected government and Council of Ministers.
