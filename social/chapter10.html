🧠 Important Concepts

1. Social Transformation

Social transformation refers to major changes in social institutions, education, customs, opportunities and relationships within society.
2. Modern Education

The expansion of modern education played an important role in creating new opportunities and spreading social awareness.
3. Women's Education

The development of women's education helped improve women's social position and increased their participation in public life.
4. Social Reform

Social reform movements challenged harmful customs and worked towards equality, education and social justice.
5. Justice Party

The Justice Party played an important role in the politics of social representation and non-Brahmin interests in the Madras Presidency.
6. Self-Respect Movement

The Self-Respect Movement promoted dignity, equality, rationalism and social reform.
7. Periyar E.V. Ramasamy

Periyar was a major social reformer who advocated rationalism, self-respect, equality and the removal of caste-based discrimination.
8. Vaikom Satyagraha

Vaikom Satyagraha was an important movement against caste-based restrictions and for social equality.
9. Temple Entry Movement

Temple entry movements challenged restrictions based on caste and demanded equal access to public religious spaces.
10. Rationalism

Rationalism encouraged people to question traditional beliefs and social practices using reason and critical thinking.
11. Social Justice

Social justice aims to provide equal opportunities, dignity and fair treatment to all sections of society.
12. Reservation

Reservation policies were introduced to improve representation and educational and employment opportunities for historically disadvantaged communities.
13. Women's Rights

Social reform movements contributed to discussions about women's education, property rights, marriage and participation in public life.
14. Eradication of Untouchability

Reformers and social movements worked towards ending untouchability and caste-based discrimination.
15. Dravidian Movement

The Dravidian movement promoted social equality, rationalism, regional identity and representation.
16. Tamil Language Development

The growth of Tamil scholarship, publishing and language-based cultural activities contributed to Tamil identity and social awareness.
17. Education and Social Change

Education helped spread new ideas and increased awareness about equality, rights and social reform.
18. Caste Reform

Social reformers challenged caste-based inequalities and worked for greater equality in society.
19. Self-Respect Marriages

Self-Respect marriages challenged certain traditional marriage practices and promoted equality and dignity.
20. Modern Tamil Society

Education, social reform, political movements and struggles for equality contributed to the transformation of modern Tamil society.

🗓️ Important Dates and Events

1916: The South Indian Liberal Federation, popularly known as the Justice Party, was established.
1917: The Justice Party became an important political force in the Madras Presidency.
1921: The Justice Party formed the government in the Madras Presidency.
1924: Vaikom Satyagraha became an important struggle against caste-based restrictions.
1925: Periyar launched the Self-Respect Movement.
1929: The Self-Respect Movement continued to promote equality, rationalism and social reform.
1930s: Social reform and movements for equality gained wider support in Tamil Nadu.
1937: Political and social debates about education, language and representation became prominent in the Madras Presidency.
1944: The Justice Party was transformed into the Dravidar Kazhagam under Periyar's leadership.
1947: India became independent and social and political transformation continued.
