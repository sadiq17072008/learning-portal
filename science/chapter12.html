🌿 Important Concepts

1. Plant Anatomy
Plant anatomy is the study of the internal structure of plants and their tissues.
2. Meristematic Tissue
Meristematic tissues consist of actively dividing cells and are responsible for plant growth.
3. Permanent Tissue
Permanent tissues are formed from meristematic tissues and generally lose the ability to divide.
4. Xylem
Xylem is a complex tissue mainly responsible for transporting water and minerals from roots to other parts of the plant.
5. Phloem
Phloem transports prepared food from leaves to different parts of the plant.
6. Transpiration
Transpiration is the loss of water in the form of water vapour from the aerial parts of plants, mainly through stomata.
7. Photosynthesis
Green plants prepare food using carbon dioxide and water in the presence of sunlight and chlorophyll.
8. Respiration
Respiration is the process by which cells release energy from food.

🧪 Important Facts

Photosynthesis: Carbon dioxide + Water → Glucose + Oxygen
Respiration: Glucose + Oxygen → Carbon dioxide + Water + Energy
Xylem: Mainly transports water and minerals.
Phloem: Transports food/materials produced by photosynthesis.
Stomata: Small pores mainly present on the epidermis of leaves that help in gaseous exchange and transpiration.
