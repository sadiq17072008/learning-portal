🧪 Important Concepts

1. Tetravalency
Carbon has four valence electrons and generally forms four covalent bonds.
2. Catenation
The ability of carbon atoms to form bonds with other carbon atoms and produce chains and rings.
3. Covalent Bond
A chemical bond formed by sharing electrons between atoms.
4. Hydrocarbons
Compounds containing only carbon and hydrogen.
5. Homologous Series
A family of organic compounds having the same functional group and similar chemical properties.
6. Functional Group
An atom or group of atoms responsible for the characteristic chemical properties of an organic compound.

🧮 Important Formulae

Methane: CH₄
Ethane: C₂H₆
Ethene: C₂H₄
Ethyne: C₂H₂
Ethanol: C₂H₅OH
Ethanoic Acid: CH₃COOH
General formula of Alkanes: CₙH₂ₙ₊₂
General formula of Alkenes: CₙH₂ₙ
General formula of Alkynes: CₙH₂ₙ₋₂
