🔬 Important Concepts

1. Xylem
Xylem is the vascular tissue responsible mainly for transporting water and dissolved minerals from the roots to the aerial parts of the plant.
2. Phloem
Phloem transports prepared food from the leaves to different parts of the plant.
3. Transpiration
Transpiration is the loss of water in the form of water vapour mainly through the stomata of leaves.
4. Translocation
Translocation is the transport of food materials through the phloem from source regions to other parts of the plant.
5. Blood
Blood is a fluid connective tissue that transports oxygen, nutrients, hormones and waste materials.
6. Heart
The heart is a muscular organ that pumps blood throughout the body.
7. Blood Vessels
Arteries, veins and capillaries are the major types of blood vessels involved in circulation.
8. Double Circulation
In double circulation, blood passes through the heart twice during one complete cycle of circulation.

🧠 Important Facts

Xylem: Transports water and minerals mainly from roots towards the aerial parts of plants.
Phloem: Transports food produced during photosynthesis to different parts of the plant.
Transpiration: Loss of water vapour from the aerial parts of plants, mainly through stomata.
Arteries: Blood vessels that generally carry blood away from the heart.
Veins: Blood vessels that generally carry blood towards the heart.
Capillaries: Very small blood vessels where exchange of materials between blood and tissues occurs.
Heart: Pumps blood through the circulatory system.
Circulation: The continuous movement of blood through the heart and blood vessels.
