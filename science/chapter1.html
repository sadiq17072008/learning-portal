🧠 Important Concepts

1. Force

Force is a push or pull acting on an object. It can change the state of rest or motion of an object or change its direction or shape.
2. Effects of Force

A force can:
• Change the state of motion.
• Change the direction of motion.
• Change the speed of an object.
• Change the shape of an object.
3. Balanced Forces

When the net force acting on an object is zero, the forces are called balanced forces.
Net Force = 0
4. Unbalanced Forces

When the net force acting on an object is not zero, the forces are called unbalanced forces.
Net Force ≠ 0
5. Inertia

Inertia is the tendency of an object to resist any change in its state of rest or uniform motion.
6. Types of Inertia

The three important types of inertia are:

• Inertia of rest
• Inertia of motion
• Inertia of direction
7. Newton's First Law

An object remains at rest or continues in uniform motion in a straight line unless acted upon by an external unbalanced force.
External unbalanced force = 0
8. Newton's Second Law

The rate of change of momentum of an object is directly proportional to the applied force and takes place in the direction of the force.
F = ma
9. Newton's Third Law

For every action, there is an equal and opposite reaction.
Action = Reaction
10. Momentum

Momentum is the product of mass and velocity.
p = mv
p = momentum
m = mass
v = velocity
11. Conservation of Momentum

In the absence of external force, the total momentum of an isolated system remains constant.
Total Initial Momentum = Total Final Momentum
12. Action and Reaction

Action and reaction forces are equal in magnitude and opposite in direction. They act on different objects.

🧮 Numerical Examples

Example 1 – Force

A body of mass 5 kg accelerates at 2 m/s². Find the force.
F = ma
F = 5 × 2
Answer: F = 10 N
Example 2 – Momentum

A body of mass 10 kg moves with a velocity of 5 m/s. Find its momentum.
p = mv
p = 10 × 5
Answer: p = 50 kg m/s
Example 3 – Finding Acceleration

A force of 20 N acts on a body of mass 4 kg. Find its acceleration.
F = ma
a = F/m
a = 20/4
Answer: a = 5 m/s²
Example 4 – Finding Mass

A force of 30 N produces an acceleration of 6 m/s². Find the mass.
m = F/a
m = 30/6
Answer: m = 5 kg
Example 5 – Momentum

A 2 kg object moves with velocity 10 m/s. Calculate its momentum.
p = mv
p = 2 × 10
Answer: p = 20 kg m/s
