🧠 Important Concepts

1. Visual Communication

Visual communication is the process of communicating information through visual means such as images, symbols, diagrams, photographs and electronic displays.
2. Communication System

A communication system is a system used to transmit information from one place to another.
3. Basic Elements of Communication

The basic elements are:

• Information source
• Transmitter
• Communication channel
• Receiver
• Destination
4. Transmitter

The transmitter converts the information into a suitable signal and sends it through the communication channel.
5. Communication Channel

The communication channel is the medium through which the signal travels from the transmitter to the receiver.
6. Receiver

The receiver receives the transmitted signal and converts it back into useful information.
7. Optical Fibre

An optical fibre is a thin flexible fibre made of glass or plastic that carries information in the form of light.
8. Total Internal Reflection

Optical fibres work mainly on the principle of total internal reflection of light.
Light travels through the fibre by repeated total internal reflection.
9. Applications of Optical Fibre

Optical fibres are used in:

• Internet communication
• Telephone communication
• Cable television
• Medical endoscopy
• Computer networking
10. Satellite Communication

Satellite communication uses artificial satellites to transmit communication signals over large distances.
11. Geostationary Satellite

A geostationary satellite appears stationary with respect to an observer on Earth because its orbital period is equal to the rotational period of Earth.
12. Remote Sensing

Remote sensing is the process of collecting information about an object or area without direct physical contact with it.
13. RADAR

RADAR stands for Radio Detection and Ranging. It is used to detect the position and distance of objects using radio waves.
14. Television Communication

Television transmits audio and visual information to viewers through communication signals.
15. Internet Communication

The Internet is a worldwide network that allows computers and other devices to exchange information.

🧮 Important Examples

Example 1 – Optical Fibre

An optical fibre transmits information using light signals.
Principle → Total Internal Reflection
Example 2 – Communication System

In a communication system, information is converted into a signal by the transmitter, transmitted through a channel and received by the receiver.
Source → Transmitter → Channel → Receiver
Example 3 – RADAR

RADAR uses radio waves to detect distant objects and determine their distance and position.
Example 4 – Remote Sensing

Satellites can collect information about Earth's surface without physically touching the objects being observed.
Example 5 – Optical Fibre Communication

Optical fibre communication is suitable for high-speed transmission because information can be carried as light signals.
