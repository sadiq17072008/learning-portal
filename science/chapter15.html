🔬 Important Concepts

1. Nervous System
The nervous system controls and coordinates various activities of the body and helps an organism respond to changes in its surroundings.
2. Neuron
A neuron is the structural and functional unit of the nervous system. It receives and transmits nerve impulses.
3. Dendrites
Dendrites are branched structures that mainly receive signals and carry them towards the cell body.
4. Axon
The axon is a long fibre that carries nerve impulses away from the cell body.
5. Central Nervous System
The central nervous system consists of the brain and spinal cord.
6. Brain
The brain is the main control centre of the nervous system. It receives information, processes it and coordinates responses.
7. Spinal Cord
The spinal cord connects the brain with peripheral nerves and plays an important role in reflex actions.
8. Reflex Action
A reflex action is a rapid, automatic response to a stimulus, generally occurring without conscious control.
9. Reflex Arc
A reflex arc is the pathway followed by a nerve impulse during a reflex action.
10. Autonomic Nervous System
The autonomic nervous system regulates involuntary activities such as heartbeat, digestion and breathing-related functions.

🧠 Important Facts

Neuron: The neuron is the basic structural and functional unit of the nervous system.
Dendrites: Receive signals and carry them towards the cell body.
Axon: Carries nerve impulses away from the cell body.
Central Nervous System: Consists mainly of the brain and spinal cord.
Cerebrum: The largest part of the brain and associated with higher mental functions.
Cerebellum: Helps coordinate muscular activity and maintain balance and posture.
Medulla Oblongata: Helps regulate several involuntary functions.
Reflex Action: A quick and automatic response to a stimulus.
Spinal Cord: Carries nerve impulses between the brain and peripheral parts of the body and participates in reflex actions.
