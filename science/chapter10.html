⚗️ Important Chemical Reactions

1. Combination Reaction

CaO + H₂O → Ca(OH)₂

Two or more substances combine to form a single product.
2. Decomposition Reaction

CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂

A single compound breaks down into two or more simpler substances.
3. Displacement Reaction

Zn + CuSO₄ → ZnSO₄ + Cu

A more reactive element displaces a less reactive element.
4. Double Displacement Reaction

Na₂SO₄ + BaCl₂ → BaSO₄ + 2NaCl

Two compounds exchange their ions.
5. Oxidation

2Cu + O₂ → 2CuO

Oxidation commonly involves addition of oxygen or removal of hydrogen.
6. Reduction

CuO + H₂ → Cu + H₂O

Reduction commonly involves removal of oxygen or addition of hydrogen.
