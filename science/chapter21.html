🧠 Important Concepts

1. Health

Health is a state of physical, mental and social well-being. Good health allows a person to perform daily activities effectively.
2. Disease

A disease is a condition that affects the normal functioning of the body or mind.
3. Infectious Diseases

Infectious diseases are caused by disease-causing organisms called pathogens and can spread from one person to another directly or indirectly.
4. Non-infectious Diseases

Non-infectious diseases do not normally spread from one person to another. They may be caused by genetic, nutritional, environmental or lifestyle factors.
5. Pathogens

Disease-causing organisms are called pathogens. They include certain bacteria, viruses, fungi and protozoans.
6. Bacterial Diseases

Some diseases are caused by bacteria. Examples include tuberculosis, typhoid and cholera.
7. Viral Diseases

Viruses can cause diseases such as influenza, dengue, measles and other viral infections.
8. Water-borne Diseases

Diseases can spread through contaminated water. Proper sanitation and safe drinking water help prevent these diseases.
9. Air-borne Diseases

Some diseases can spread through droplets or particles released into the air by infected persons.
10. Vector-borne Diseases

Some diseases are transmitted by organisms called vectors. Mosquitoes are important vectors of several diseases.
11. Immunity

Immunity is the ability of the body to resist infection and protect itself against disease-causing organisms.
12. Vaccination

Vaccination helps the immune system develop protection against particular infectious diseases.
13. Prevention

Diseases can be prevented through personal hygiene, sanitation, safe food and water, vaccination, vector control and healthy habits.
14. Healthy Lifestyle

A healthy lifestyle includes balanced nutrition, regular physical activity, adequate sleep, personal hygiene and avoidance of harmful habits.

🧮 Important Examples

Example 1 – Infectious Disease

A disease caused by a pathogen and capable of spreading between individuals is called an infectious disease.
Pathogen → Infection → Disease
Example 2 – Prevention

Regular hand washing, safe drinking water and proper sanitation can reduce the spread of many infectious diseases.
Example 3 – Immunity

The immune system identifies harmful organisms and helps protect the body against infections.
Example 4 – Vaccination

Vaccines stimulate the immune system to develop protection against particular infectious diseases.
Example 5 – Healthy Living

A balanced diet, exercise, adequate sleep and good hygiene are important for maintaining good health.
