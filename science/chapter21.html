🔬 Important Concepts

1. Health
Health refers to a state of physical, mental and social well-being. Maintaining a healthy lifestyle helps prevent many diseases and disorders.
2. Drug Abuse
Repeated or excessive use of drugs can lead to dependence and harmful physical and psychological effects.
3. Tobacco Addiction
Nicotine in tobacco can cause dependence and has harmful effects on the cardiovascular and respiratory systems.
4. Alcohol Abuse
Prolonged excessive alcohol consumption can affect the nervous system and damage organs including the liver.
5. De-addiction
De-addiction involves appropriate medical, psychological and social support to help a person overcome substance dependence.
6. Diabetes Mellitus
Diabetes mellitus is a chronic metabolic disorder associated with elevated blood glucose levels.
7. Type 1 Diabetes
Type 1 diabetes is associated with inadequate insulin production and generally requires insulin treatment.
8. Type 2 Diabetes
Type 2 diabetes is commonly associated with insulin resistance and lifestyle-related risk factors.
9. Obesity
Obesity occurs when energy intake over time exceeds energy expenditure, leading to excessive accumulation of body fat.
10. Body Mass Index
BMI is calculated as:

BMI = Weight (kg) / Height² (m²)
11. Coronary Heart Disease
Coronary heart disease can occur when the blood supply to heart muscles is insufficient.
12. Atherosclerosis
Atherosclerosis involves the buildup of fatty deposits in arterial walls, which can reduce blood flow.
13. Cancer
Cancer involves abnormal and uncontrolled cell growth. Different types are classified according to the tissues or cells involved.
14. Carcinoma
Carcinoma is a cancer arising from epithelial cells.
15. Leukemia
Leukemia is a cancer involving blood-forming tissues and abnormal blood cell production.
16. HIV and AIDS
HIV is a virus that attacks the immune system. AIDS is the advanced condition associated with severe damage to immune function.
17. Disease Prevention
Healthy diet, physical activity, avoiding tobacco and harmful substance use, appropriate medical care and awareness can help reduce health risks.

🧠 Important Facts

Nicotine: Nicotine is the addictive component of tobacco and can stimulate adrenaline secretion.
No Tobacco Day: World No Tobacco Day is observed on May 31.
Alcohol: Excessive alcohol consumption can cause serious health problems including liver damage and cirrhosis.
Diabetes: Diabetes mellitus is a chronic metabolic disorder involving abnormal blood glucose regulation.
Obesity: Obesity is associated with excessive accumulation of body fat.
BMI: BMI is calculated using body weight and height.
Heart Disease: Coronary heart disease can result from insufficient blood supply to the heart muscles.
Carcinoma: Cancer of epithelial cells is called carcinoma.
Cancer Cells: Rapidly dividing cancer cells can be particularly sensitive to radiation treatment.
HIV: HIV attacks cells of the immune system and can lead to AIDS if the infection progresses.
