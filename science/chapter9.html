🧮 Important Concepts & Formulae

Mass Percentage:
Mass % = (Mass of solute / Mass of solution) × 100
Volume Percentage:
Volume % = (Volume of solute / Volume of solution) × 100
Solution:
Solution = Solute + Solvent
Solubility:
Maximum amount of solute that can dissolve in a given amount of solvent at a specified temperature.
