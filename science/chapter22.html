🧠 Important Concepts

1. Environment

Environment includes all living and non-living components surrounding organisms and influencing their life.
2. Environmental Management

Environmental management involves the responsible use, protection and conservation of natural resources while maintaining ecological balance.
3. Natural Resources

Natural resources are materials and substances obtained from nature and used by humans.

Examples include water, forests, soil, minerals and energy resources.
4. Renewable Resources

Renewable resources can be naturally replenished within a relatively short period.

Examples:
• Solar energy
• Wind energy
• Water
• Biomass
5. Non-renewable Resources

Non-renewable resources are available in limited quantities and cannot be replaced quickly.

Examples:
• Coal
• Petroleum
• Natural gas
• Minerals
6. Biodiversity

Biodiversity refers to the variety of living organisms present in a particular region or on Earth.
7. Conservation

Conservation means the protection and careful management of natural resources and biodiversity for present and future generations.
8. Deforestation

Deforestation is the large-scale removal of forests for purposes such as agriculture, construction and industrial development.
9. Afforestation

Afforestation is the process of establishing forests on land that was previously not covered by forests.
10. Pollution

Pollution is the undesirable change in the physical, chemical or biological characteristics of the environment.
11. Waste Management

Waste management includes the collection, treatment, recycling and safe disposal of waste.
12. Sustainable Development

Sustainable development means meeting present needs without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their needs.
13. Reduce, Reuse and Recycle

The three important principles of waste reduction are:

• Reduce consumption
• Reuse materials
• Recycle waste
14. Rainwater Harvesting

Rainwater harvesting is the collection and storage of rainwater for future use and groundwater recharge.

🧮 Environmental Examples

Example 1 – Renewable Energy

Solar energy is considered renewable because sunlight is naturally replenished.
Solar Energy → Renewable Resource
Example 2 – Water Conservation

Rainwater harvesting can collect rainwater and help recharge groundwater.
Rainwater → Collection → Storage / Recharge
Example 3 – Waste Management

Separating biodegradable and non-biodegradable waste makes waste management easier.
Waste Segregation → Treatment → Safe Disposal
Example 4 – Conservation

Protecting forests helps conserve biodiversity, soil and water resources.
Forest Conservation → Biodiversity Protection
Example 5 – Sustainable Development

Using resources carefully while protecting the environment supports sustainable development.
Present Needs + Future Protection
