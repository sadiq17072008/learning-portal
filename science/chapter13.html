🔬 Important Concepts

1. Animal Tissue
A tissue is a group of cells that are structurally and functionally similar and perform a specific function.
2. Epithelial Tissue
Epithelial tissue forms the covering or lining of many body surfaces and organs. It also performs protective, absorptive and secretory functions.
3. Connective Tissue
Connective tissue connects, supports, protects and binds different parts of the body.
4. Muscular Tissue
Muscular tissue is specialised for contraction and helps in movement of body parts.
5. Nervous Tissue
Nervous tissue receives and transmits electrical signals and helps coordinate activities of the body.
6. Blood
Blood is a fluid connective tissue that transports gases, nutrients, hormones and waste materials.
7. Bone
Bone is a hard connective tissue that provides support, protection and a framework for the body.
8. Neuron
A neuron is the structural and functional unit of nervous tissue. It receives and transmits nerve impulses.

🧠 Important Facts

Epithelial Tissue: Covers body surfaces and lines internal organs and cavities.
Connective Tissue: Supports, connects and protects different tissues and organs.
Blood: A fluid connective tissue containing plasma and blood cells.
Cartilage: A flexible connective tissue that provides support in various parts of the body.
Muscular Tissue: Responsible for movement through contraction.
Nervous Tissue: Responsible for receiving and transmitting nerve impulses.
Neuron: The basic structural and functional unit of the nervous system.
