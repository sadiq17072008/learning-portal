📘 Prose – The Last Lesson

About the Lesson

The Last Lesson presents the emotional experience of attending a final lesson in the French language after an order that German would be taught in the schools of Alsace and Lorraine.
Franz

Franz is a schoolboy who initially does not want to attend his lesson. The final lesson makes him realise the importance of learning and language.
M. Hamel

M. Hamel is Franz's French teacher. He conducts the final French lesson with dignity and deep emotion.
Importance of Language

The lesson presents language as an important part of identity, culture and freedom.
Main Message

We should value education and our language instead of realising their importance only after losing them.

🎵 Poem – No Men Are Foreign

Poet

James Falconer Kirkup wrote the poem No Men Are Foreign.
Central Idea

The poem teaches that human beings are essentially alike, regardless of country, language or outward identity.
Brotherhood

All people share the same earth, breathe the same air and depend upon the same basic elements of life.
Against War

The poet warns that hatred and war ultimately harm humanity itself.
Main Message

No person should be treated as a stranger or enemy merely because they belong to another country.

📗 Supplementary – The Little Hero of Holland

About the Story

The story tells about a young Dutch boy who notices water leaking through a hole in a dike.
The Boy's Action

The boy puts his finger into the hole and remains there to prevent the water from entering until help arrives.
Courage

Although he is only a child, he shows courage and responsibility when his community is in danger.
Main Theme

Courage, presence of mind, responsibility and selflessness are the central ideas of the story.

✍️ Grammar and Language Practice

Tense:

"Franz walked to school."

Answer: Simple Past Tense
Modal: We ___ respect every language.

Answer: should
Article: He saw ___ unusual situation.

Answer: an
Preposition: The boy stood ___ the wall.

Answer: near
Subject-Verb Agreement:

The teacher ___ the lesson.

Answer: teaches
Active and Passive Voice:

Active: "The boy saved the people."

Passive: "The people were saved by the boy."
