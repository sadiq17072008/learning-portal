📘 Prose – The Dying Detective

About the Story

The Dying Detective is a detective story involving Sherlock Holmes, Dr Watson and a dangerous criminal mystery.
Sherlock Holmes

Sherlock Holmes uses observation, intelligence and careful planning to solve the mystery.
Dr Watson

Dr Watson is Holmes's close friend. He becomes deeply concerned when Holmes appears to be seriously ill.
Culverton Smith

Culverton Smith is associated with the mysterious disease and becomes an important part of Holmes's plan.
Main Idea

The story demonstrates the power of intelligence, observation, courage and careful investigation.

🎵 Poem – The House on Elm Street

Central Idea

The poem describes a mysterious house and the feelings and questions it creates in the minds of people.
Mystery

The house is presented as unusual and mysterious, creating curiosity about what happens inside it.
Imagination

The poem encourages the reader to imagine the unknown and think beyond what can immediately be seen.
Atmosphere

The descriptions create a mysterious and slightly unsettling atmosphere.
Main Message

The unknown can create curiosity, imagination and different interpretations in people's minds.

📗 Supplementary – A Dilemma

About the Story

A Dilemma is a story involving Tom, his father, Uncle Philip and Professor Clinch. The story presents a difficult moral and personal decision.
Uncle Philip

Uncle Philip presents Tom with a challenging situation that forces him to think carefully about his decision.
Tom

Tom has to deal with an unexpected problem and must decide how to respond to the situation.
The Dilemma

The central conflict involves choosing between difficult alternatives and understanding their consequences.
Main Theme

The story highlights decision-making, responsibility, consequences and moral conflict.

✍️ Grammar and Language Practice

Tense:

"Holmes examined the evidence."

Answer: Simple Past Tense
Modal: Watson ___ help Holmes.

Answer: must
Article: Holmes was ___ intelligent detective.

Answer: an
Preposition: The house stood ___ Elm Street.

Answer: on
Subject-Verb Agreement:

The detective ___ the evidence.

Answer: examines
Active and Passive Voice:

Active: "Holmes solved the mystery."

Passive: "The mystery was solved by Holmes."
