📘 Prose – The Night the Ghost Got In

About the Prose

The lesson is a humorous account by James Thurber. The narrator hears noises in the house at night and believes that a ghost has entered.
Important Characters

The narrator, his brother, Mother, and the police are important characters in the humorous incident.
Main Idea

The lesson uses humour and misunderstanding to show how an ordinary night can turn into a confusing and amusing event.
Literary Feature

Humour, exaggeration, misunderstanding and unexpected situations make the narrative entertaining.

🎵 Poem – The Grumble Family

Central Idea

The poem humorously describes people who complain constantly and suggests that a negative attitude can spread from one person to another.
Theme

The poem highlights the effect of habitual complaining and encourages a more positive attitude towards life.
Poetic Style

The poem uses humour, repetition and playful language to communicate its message.

📗 Supplementary – Zigzag

About Zigzag

Zigzag is a humorous supplementary story involving a family and a surprising bird.
Dr. Krishnan

Dr. Krishnan is the doctor in the story and reacts to the unusual events surrounding Zigzag.
Maya and Arvind

Maya and Arvind are children who become interested in Zigzag and its unusual abilities.
Zigzag

Zigzag is the unusual bird whose behaviour and abilities create the central humour of the story.
Main Idea

The story develops humour through the family's expectations and Zigzag's unexpected behaviour.

✍️ Grammar and Language Practice

Synonym: Choose the word closest in meaning to complain.

Answer: grumble
Antonym: Choose the opposite of positive.

Answer: negative
Phrasal Verb: "Look after" means:

Answer: take care of
Sentence: Identify the tense:

"The family heard a strange noise."

Answer: Simple Past Tense
Sentence Transformation: Change the sentence into negative:

"He likes the bird."

Answer: He does not like the bird.
