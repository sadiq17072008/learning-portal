📘 Prose – The Attic

About the Prose

The lesson revolves around an attic and the memories connected with it. The attic becomes a place where the past and present come together.
Human Values

The lesson highlights important human values such as love, care, respect, forgiveness and faith.
Memories

The attic represents memories and objects from the past. Such memories help people understand their relationships and experiences.
Central Message

The lesson reminds us about the importance of human relationships, memories, faith and compassion.

🎵 Poem – The Ant and the Cricket

Central Idea

The poem contrasts the hardworking ant with the careless cricket and teaches the importance of preparing for the future.
The Ant

The ant works hard and stores food during the good season so that it will have enough during winter.
The Cricket

The cricket spends the warm season singing instead of preparing for the future and later faces hardship.
Moral

The poem teaches that hard work, planning and saving are important for a secure future.

📗 Supplementary – The Aged Mother

About the Story

The story focuses on an aged mother and the love between a mother and her son.
The Son

The son deeply loves and respects his mother and is unwilling to abandon her.
The Aged Mother

The aged mother represents the love, sacrifice and wisdom of parents.
Main Theme

The story highlights filial love, respect for elders, compassion and human values.
Lesson

Parents deserve love, care and respect, especially when they become old.

✍️ Grammar and Language Practice

Tense: Identify the tense:

"The ant stored food."

Answer: Simple Past Tense
Modal: Choose the correct modal:

The cricket ___ have prepared for winter.

Answer: should
Article: Choose the correct article:

The ant was ___ hardworking creature.

Answer: a
Preposition: The cricket asked the ant ___ food.

Answer: for
Adjective: Identify the adjective:

"The old mother was wise."

Answer: old
