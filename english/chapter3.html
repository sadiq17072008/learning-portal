📘 Prose – Empowered Women Navigating the World

About the Prose

The lesson focuses on women who challenge traditional limitations, develop confidence and demonstrate their ability to succeed in different fields.
Central Idea

Women can overcome social, physical and professional barriers through courage, determination and education.
Empowerment

Empowerment means giving people the confidence, opportunity and ability to make decisions and achieve their goals.
Main Message

The lesson encourages equality, confidence and opportunities for women to participate actively in society.

🎵 Poem – I am Every Woman

Central Idea

The poem celebrates the strength, confidence and resilience of women.
Theme

The poem presents women as courageous individuals capable of facing difficulties and overcoming challenges.
Strength of Women

The poem highlights determination, courage, self-confidence and the ability to face difficult situations.
Message

Women should be respected, encouraged and recognised for their abilities and achievements.

📗 Supplementary – The Story of Mulan

About Mulan

Mulan is a courageous young woman who takes her father's place in the army because he is old and unable to fight.
Mulan's Courage

Mulan disguises herself and joins the army to protect her father and serve her country.
Determination

Mulan proves that courage, discipline and ability are not limited by gender.
Main Theme

The story highlights courage, family love, patriotism, sacrifice and gender equality.
Lesson

True ability is demonstrated through actions, courage and determination rather than gender.

✍️ Grammar and Language Practice

Modal: Choose the correct modal:

Mulan ___ protect her father.

Answer: could
Active Voice: Change into passive voice:

"Mulan joined the army."

Answer: The army was joined by Mulan.
Article: Choose the correct article:

Mulan was ___ brave woman.

Answer: a
Preposition: Mulan went ___ the army.

Answer: to
Tense: Identify the tense:

"Mulan joined the army."

Answer: Simple Past Tense
