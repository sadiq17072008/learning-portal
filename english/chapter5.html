💻 Prose — Tech Bloomers

The prose lesson highlights how modern technology can empower people with disabilities and help them communicate, study, work and become more independent.

Central idea: Technology can remove barriers and provide opportunities for people with special needs.
David: David uses assistive technology to communicate, control devices and participate more independently in daily activities.
Communication: Assistive communication devices can allow a person who cannot speak normally to communicate through technology.
Eye-gaze technology: Eye movements can be used to control a computer interface and select required icons.
Independence: Technology can help people perform activities, access education and communicate with others.

⚙️ Technology and Accessibility

Assistive technology refers to devices and systems that help people overcome physical or communication difficulties.
Technology can provide access to education, entertainment, communication and employment.
The lesson demonstrates that technology is not merely entertainment; it can become a powerful tool for inclusion.

📝 Tech Bloomers — Quick Revision

Theme: Technology and empowerment.
Important idea: Technology can reduce barriers faced by people with disabilities.
Communication: Special devices can help users communicate using alternative methods.
Education: Technology can provide access to learning materials and curriculum.
Independence: Assistive devices can increase confidence and self-reliance.

🤖 Poem — The Secret of the Machines

The poem presents machines as powerful creations of human beings. It describes what machines can do and reminds readers that machines depend upon their creators.

Theme: The power and usefulness of machines.
Human creation: Machines are created by human intelligence, knowledge and engineering.
Power: Machines can perform difficult tasks quickly and efficiently.
Dependence: Machines require human knowledge, control and maintenance.
Message: Technology is powerful, but humans remain the creators and controllers of machines.

📖 The Secret of the Machines — Quick Revision

Subject: Machines and their abilities.
Creator: Human beings create machines.
Purpose: Machines are designed to perform useful tasks.
Important message: Machines may be powerful, but they remain dependent on humans.

📰 Supplementary — A Day in 2889 of an American Journalist

The supplementary lesson presents an imaginative vision of life in the future. It describes advanced technology and the way communication and daily life could change in a technologically developed society.

Future society: The story imagines a technologically advanced world.
Communication: Future technology makes communication extremely fast and convenient.
Transportation: Advanced technology changes the way people travel.
Journalism: The journalist observes and reports the features of the imagined future society.
Science fiction: The story combines imaginative future technology with human life.

🌟 Unit 5 — Overall Theme

Technology: The three lessons explore different aspects of technology and machines.
Accessibility: Technology can help people with special needs participate more fully in society.
Machines: Machines are human creations designed to perform useful functions.
Future: Scientific and technological development can transform everyday life.

📌 Important Vocabulary

Word Meaning
Technology Application of scientific knowledge for practical purposes.
Assistive Helping a person perform a task more easily.
Communicator A person or device used for communication.
Accessible Easy to reach, use or access.
Independent Able to act or function without depending on others.
Competent Having the necessary ability or skill.
Innovation A new idea, method or invention.
Automation Use of machines or technology to perform tasks automatically.
