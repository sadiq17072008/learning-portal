🧠 Important Concepts

1. Reading

Reading comprehension means understanding the meaning, ideas, facts and emotions expressed in a passage.
2. Vocabulary

Vocabulary refers to the words a person knows and uses. Learning synonyms, antonyms, prefixes and suffixes helps improve vocabulary.
3. Synonym

A synonym is a word having the same or nearly the same meaning as another word.
4. Antonym

An antonym is a word having an opposite meaning.
5. Prefix

A prefix is added to the beginning of a word to modify its meaning.
6. Suffix

A suffix is added to the end of a word to modify its form or meaning.
7. Grammar

Grammar is the system of rules used to form correct sentences.
8. Tense

Tense shows the time of an action or event.
9. Present Tense

Present tense is generally used for actions happening now, regular actions and facts.
10. Past Tense

Past tense is used for actions that happened in the past.
11. Future Tense

Future tense is used for actions that are expected to happen later.
12. Phrasal Verb

A phrasal verb is formed using a verb together with one or more particles.
13. Comprehension

A comprehension exercise tests how well a student understands a given passage.
14. Writing Skill

Good writing requires appropriate vocabulary, grammar, organisation, clarity and correct sentence construction.
15. Poem Appreciation

Appreciating a poem involves understanding its theme, imagery, message, poetic devices and emotions.

✍️ Language Practice

Example 1: Choose the synonym of brave.

Answer: courageous
Example 2: Choose the antonym of ancient.

Answer: modern
Example 3: Add a suitable prefix:

___ possible

Answer: impossible
Example 4: Identify the tense:

"She visited Chennai yesterday."

Answer: Simple Past Tense
Example 5: Identify the tense:

"They are studying English."

Answer: Present Continuous Tense
Example 6: Identify the phrasal verb:

"Please look after your brother."

Answer: look after
