🧠 Important Algebra Formulas

1. Square of a Sum
(a + b)² = a² + 2ab + b²
2. Square of a Difference
(a − b)² = a² − 2ab + b²
3. Difference of Squares
a² − b² = (a − b)(a + b)
4. Product of Sum and Difference
(a + b)(a − b) = a² − b²
5. Cube of a Sum
(a + b)³ = a³ + 3a²b + 3ab² + b³
6. Cube of a Difference
(a − b)³ = a³ − 3a²b + 3ab² − b³
7. Sum of Cubes
a³ + b³ = (a + b)(a² − ab + b²)
8. Difference of Cubes
a³ − b³ = (a − b)(a² + ab + b²)
9. Quadratic Equation
ax² + bx + c = 0
10. Quadratic Formula
x = [−b ± √(b² − 4ac)] / 2a
11. Discriminant
D = b² − 4ac
12. Sum of Roots
α + β = −b/a
13. Product of Roots
αβ = c/a

✏️ Solved Numerical Examples

Example 1: Expand: (x + 5)²

(x + 5)²
= x² + 2(x)(5) + 5²
= x² + 10x + 25
Example 2: Factorise: x² − 25

x² − 5²
= (x − 5)(x + 5)
Example 3: Solve: 2x + 7 = 19

2x = 19 − 7
2x = 12
x = 6
Example 4: Solve: x² − 5x + 6 = 0

(x − 2)(x − 3) = 0
Therefore,
x = 2 or 3
Example 5: Solve: x² − 9 = 0

x² = 9
x = ±3
