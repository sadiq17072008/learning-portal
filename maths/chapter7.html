🧠 Important Mensuration Formulas

1. Cuboid

Volume = l × b × h
Total Surface Area = 2(lb + bh + hl)
Lateral Surface Area = 2h(l + b)
2. Cube

Volume = a³
Total Surface Area = 6a²
Lateral Surface Area = 4a²
3. Cylinder

Volume = πr²h
Curved Surface Area = 2πrh
Total Surface Area = 2πr(r + h)
4. Cone

Volume = 1/3 πr²h
Slant Height: l = √(r² + h²)
Curved Surface Area = πrl
Total Surface Area = πr(l + r)
5. Sphere

Volume = 4/3 πr³
Surface Area = 4πr²
6. Hemisphere

Volume = 2/3 πr³
Curved Surface Area = 2πr²
Total Surface Area = 3πr²
7. Circle

Area = πr²
Circumference = 2πr
8. Rectangle

Area = l × b
Perimeter = 2(l + b)
9. Triangle

Area = 1/2 × base × height

✏️ Solved Numerical Examples

Example 1: Find the area of a rectangle whose length is 12 cm and breadth is 8 cm.

Area = l × b
= 12 × 8
Answer = 96 cm²
Example 2: Find the volume of a cuboid with length 10 cm, breadth 5 cm and height 4 cm.

Volume = l × b × h
= 10 × 5 × 4
Answer = 200 cm³
Example 3: Find the volume of a cylinder with radius 7 cm and height 10 cm. Take π = 22/7.

Volume = πr²h
= 22/7 × 7 × 7 × 10
Answer = 1540 cm³
Example 4: Find the volume of a sphere with radius 3 cm.

Volume = 4/3 πr³
= 4/3 × π × 3³
= 36π
Answer = 36π cm³
Example 5: A cube has side 6 cm. Find its total surface area.

TSA = 6a²
= 6 × 6²
= 6 × 36
Answer = 216 cm²
Example 6: Find the curved surface area of a cylinder with radius 7 cm and height 10 cm. Take π = 22/7.

CSA = 2πrh
= 2 × 22/7 × 7 × 10
Answer = 440 cm²
Example 7: A cone has radius 3 cm and height 4 cm. Find its slant height.

l = √(r² + h²)
= √(3² + 4²)
= √25
Answer = 5 cm
Example 8: Find the circumference of a circle with radius 7 cm. Take π = 22/7.

C = 2πr
= 2 × 22/7 × 7
Answer = 44 cm
