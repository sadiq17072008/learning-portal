🧠 Important Geometry Formulas

1. Pythagoras Theorem
In a right-angled triangle:
Hypotenuse² = Base² + Perpendicular²
2. Hypotenuse
c = √(a² + b²)
3. Similar Triangles
Corresponding sides of similar triangles are proportional.
4. Area of Triangle
Area = ½ × Base × Height
5. Area Ratio of Similar Triangles
Area₁ / Area₂ = (Corresponding Side₁ / Corresponding Side₂)²
6. Tangent Property
Radius is perpendicular to the tangent at the point of contact.
7. Tangents from an External Point
Tangents drawn from the same external point are equal.
8. Circle Circumference
C = 2πr
9. Circle Area
A = πr²
10. Diameter
d = 2r

✏️ Solved Numerical Examples

Example 1: Find the hypotenuse of a right triangle whose sides are 6 cm and 8 cm.

c² = 6² + 8²
c² = 36 + 64
c² = 100
c = 10 cm
Example 2: Find the area of a triangle with base 12 cm and height 8 cm.

Area = ½ × 12 × 8
Area = 48 cm²
Example 3: A circle has radius 7 cm. Find its circumference.

C = 2πr
= 2 × 22/7 × 7
= 44 cm
Example 4: Two similar triangles have corresponding sides 4 cm and 8 cm. Find their area ratio.

Area ratio = (4/8)²
= (1/2)²
= 1 : 4
Example 5: A tangent from an external point has length 12 cm. Find the length of the other tangent from the same point.

Tangents from the same external point are equal.
Therefore, other tangent = 12 cm
