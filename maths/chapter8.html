🧠 Important Formulas

1. Arithmetic Mean

Mean = Sum of observations / Number of observations

x̄ = Σx / n
2. Mean with Frequency

Mean = Σfx / Σf

where f = frequency and x = observation.
3. Median

Arrange the observations in ascending order.

If n is odd:
Median = value of (n + 1) / 2 th observation

If n is even:
Median = average of n/2 th and (n/2 + 1) th observations.
4. Mode

Mode is the observation that occurs most frequently.
5. Probability

P(E) = Number of favourable outcomes / Total number of equally likely outcomes
6. Probability Range

0 ≤ P(E) ≤ 1

Impossible event → P(E) = 0
Certain event → P(E) = 1
7. Complementary Event

P(not E) = 1 - P(E)
8. Sum of Frequencies

N = Σf
9. Range

Range = Largest observation - Smallest observation

✏️ Solved Numerical Examples

Example 1: Find the mean of 5, 10, 15, 20 and 25.

Mean = (5 + 10 + 15 + 20 + 25) / 5
= 75 / 5
Answer = 15
Example 2: Find the median of 4, 7, 2, 9 and 5.

Arranged data:
2, 4, 5, 7, 9

Middle value = 5
Answer = 5
Example 3: Find the mode of:
2, 3, 4, 3, 5, 3, 6

3 occurs most frequently.
Answer = 3
Example 4: A die is thrown once. Find the probability of getting an even number.

Even outcomes = 2, 4, 6
Favourable outcomes = 3
Total outcomes = 6
P(E) = 3/6
Answer = 1/2
Example 5: A coin is tossed once. Find the probability of getting a head.

Favourable outcomes = 1
Total outcomes = 2
Answer = 1/2
Example 6: Find the range of:
12, 18, 25, 9, 30

Range = 30 - 9
Answer = 21
