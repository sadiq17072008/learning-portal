🧠 Important Coordinate Geometry Formulas

1. Distance Formula

Distance between A(x₁,y₁) and B(x₂,y₂):

d = √[(x₂ − x₁)² + (y₂ − y₁)²]
2. Midpoint Formula

M = ((x₁+x₂)/2 , (y₁+y₂)/2)
3. Section Formula

If P divides AB internally in the ratio m:n:

P = ((mx₂+nx₁)/(m+n), (my₂+ny₁)/(m+n))
4. Slope Formula

m = (y₂−y₁)/(x₂−x₁)
5. Area of Triangle

Area = ½ |x₁(y₂−y₃) + x₂(y₃−y₁) + x₃(y₁−y₂)|
6. Parallel Lines

For parallel lines:
m₁ = m₂
7. Horizontal Line

Slope = 0
8. Vertical Line

Slope is undefined.
9. Collinear Points

Three points are collinear when the area of the triangle formed by them is zero.

✏️ Solved Numerical Examples

Example 1: Find the distance between A(2,3) and B(6,6).

d = √[(6−2)² + (6−3)²]
d = √[4² + 3²]
d = √25
d = 5 units
Example 2: Find the midpoint of A(2,4) and B(8,10).

M = ((2+8)/2 , (4+10)/2)
M = (10/2 , 14/2)
M = (5,7)
Example 3: Find the distance between A(0,0) and B(3,4).

d = √[(3−0)² + (4−0)²]
d = √(9+16)
d = √25
d = 5 units
Example 4: Find the midpoint of A(-4,2) and B(6,8).

M = ((−4+6)/2 , (2+8)/2)
M = (2/2 , 10/2)
M = (1,5)
Example 5: Find the slope of the line joining A(2,3) and B(6,11).

m = (11−3)/(6−2)
m = 8/4
m = 2
Example 6: Find the area of the triangle whose vertices are (0,0), (4,0), and (0,3).

Area = ½ × base × height
= ½ × 4 × 3
= 6 square units
Example 7: Find the point dividing the line joining A(2,4) and B(8,10) internally in the ratio 1:2.

x = (1×8 + 2×2)/(1+2)
x = 12/3 = 4

y = (1×10 + 2×4)/(1+2)
y = 18/3 = 6
Point = (4,6)
